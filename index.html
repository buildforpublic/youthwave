Who We Are

Pertubuhan Gelombang Muda Malaysia, famously known as YouthWave Malaysia, was founded on April 11, 2020, by Ms. Lilitha Govindasamy.

We are a dedicated youth NGO based in Kuala Lumpur, representing the voices of young people on current national issues and fighting for remarkable changes in our society.

💡

Our Vision

To empower individuals, particularly from vulnerable or underserved communities, by helping them generate additional sources of income outside of their primary employment or livelihood.

🚀

Our Mission

To improve financial stability and quality of life by providing the tools, skills, and opportunities needed to create sustainable side incomes.

What We Do

Driving change through actionable initiatives.

🤝

Empower Youth

We provide a platform for Malaysian youths to voice their perspectives on critical national issues and advocate for meaningful social change.

📈

Build Skills

From resume mastery to entrepreneurship workshops, we equip young people with the practical skills needed to thrive in the modern economy.

💼

Create Opportunities

We connect youths with resources to generate sustainable side incomes, ensuring financial stability and a better quality of life.
